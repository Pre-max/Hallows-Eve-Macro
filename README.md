HALLOWS MACRO - quick start
===========================

1. Double-click HallowsMacro.exe and click YES on the administrator prompt.
   (Roblox ignores keys and clicks from programs that are not run as administrator.)

2. First time only: press "How to set up" inside the program and follow the steps.
   Everything you pick is saved next to the program, so you only do it once.

3. Press START, then click on the Roblox window during the 5 second countdown.
   F8 or the red EMERGENCY STOP button stops everything instantly.

You do NOT need to install Python or Tesseract OCR. The text reader is already inside the program.

What you need
- Windows 10 or 11
- Roblox windowed, borderless or fullscreen (not exclusive fullscreen), on your main monitor

If Windows or your antivirus warns about the file
- Programs that press keys and click the mouse are often flagged, even harmless ones.
  On the blue "Windows protected your PC" screen click "More info" > "Run anyway".
- The macro never connects to the internet and never collects data. It only looks at your screen
  and presses keys / clicks the mouse. Only run a copy you got from someone you trust.

Fair play
- Automation can be against Roblox's Terms of Use or a game's own rules, and nobody can promise this
  tool is allowed or that it will never lead to a warning or ban. Use it at your own risk.

Problems?
- Use the Test buttons to see what the macro sees (Test OCR, Test Fish, Test UI, Test Slots, Test Store).
- If the macro stops by itself, the reason is shown at the bottom of the window and saved in
  macro_error.log next to the program.
